Few facts about the world we live in

Earth is our home planet, the third planet from the sun, and the only planet in our solar system that supports life. It's a rocky planet with an atmosphere that protects us from harmful radiation. Earth formed about 4.5 billion years ago from gas and dust. It's the largest and densest of the four rocky planets in our solar system. Earth's moon formed when a giant impact threw rock, gas, and dust into space.
